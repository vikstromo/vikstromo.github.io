Current Ideas

    Working on a device called BIAS. Please see the dedicated space for more info. My ambition is to have a proper documentation for this project from now on. So I will upload audio demos and write stuff when I have the time to do so.
  • Sonic Storytelling
    During 2023-2025 I took my masters degree at Royal College of Music in Stockholm. During that time I explored ways to narrate stories from a sawmill village called Båtskärsnäs. I created two pieces where I mixed poetry, sound design, music and live improvisation. Hopefully I will find a way to perform this soon again.
