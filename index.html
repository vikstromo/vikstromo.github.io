Current Ideas

  • Working on a device called BIAS. The idea is to have a self contained listener and player in improvised sets. It works with synths designed by me, inspired by classical electronic sounds. Instead of processing live audio with typical effects(reverb, delay, pitch shift and distortion), BIAS is analyzing what I play both in real time, but also works with memory storage. It shifts behaviour based on what I have been doing recently and what I am doing now. This project aims to challenge problems I think occur when combining woodwind and electronics when working with free improvisation as a method. Instead of having the "electronic setup" imitate behaviour or let the instrument act as a midi-controller, the system is acting as it's own agent. Triggering me with ideas that triggers the syste with new ideas. It is an ongoing project in a early stage. Hopefully documentation will be available here in a near future .
  • During 2023-2025 I did a master's degree at KMH in Stockholm. My project was about developing a sonic storytelling practice as an artist. During this program I completed to live performances which combined audio drama, me on clarinet and bass clarinet, and real time processing. The project explored ways of presenting an audio drama live so that improvisation could be used as a method for me to participate in the audio drama in real time. The first project was performed with a quadraphonic speaker setup and the second was performed using the sound dome at KMH which is a 29 + 4 speaker system.
